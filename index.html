    Frequently asked

    Wetsuit sales only
    Do you sell surfboards as well?

    No. We sell wetsuits only, so every suit we hold is checked and cleaned before it goes out. For boards, ask us on WhatsApp and we will point you to a trusted shop in the area.

    Which thickness should I buy?

    If you surf here year round: a 4/3mm covers October to May and a 3/2mm covers the summer. June to September: 2/2mm or 3/2mm. December to February, or if you feel the cold: 5/4mm semi-dry.

    How do I pay, and can I try the suit first?

    If you are in Tamraght or Taghazout, yes — we meet in person and you try it on before you pay. For delivery elsewhere in Morocco you pay on delivery, or by transfer in advance if you prefer.

    Do you deliver across Morocco?

    Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your city and we will confirm the delivery time and any delivery cost before you order.

    Are the suits new or used?

    Both, and each listing says so in its description. Used suits are fully checked and cleaned before sale, which is why they cost less. Ask us if you want to know the condition of a particular suit.

    What if the size is wrong?

    Tell us straight away. If you are nearby we will swap it for another size, and if you are further away in Morocco we will help you arrange sending it back.
