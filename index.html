    Frequently asked

    Wetsuit rentals only
    Do you rent surfboards as well?

    No. SURF CREW Agadir rents wetsuits only, so every suit in the quiver is kept in rental condition and properly sanitised. For boards, ask us on WhatsApp and we will point you to a trusted shop in Aourir or Tamraght.

    Which thickness should I book for my trip?

    June to September: 2/2mm or 3/2mm. October to May: 4/3mm. December to February, or if you feel the cold: 5/4mm semi-dry.

    How do I pay and what is the deposit?

    Cash in MAD on pickup, or arrange a transfer with us in advance. We hold a photo ID or your accommodation booking as a deposit and return it with the suit.

    Can I collect the suit the evening before?

    Yes, collection from 18:00 the day before costs nothing extra when the suit is free, so you can be in the water at first light.

    Does the site work without internet at the beach?

    Yes. Install it on your phone from your browser menu (“Add to Home Screen”). The catalogue then opens offline from your last visit, and your WhatsApp order queues until you have signal again.
